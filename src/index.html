Rthoptera Desk
A quick guide to finding your way around — start by loading audio, then work your way across the tabs.
1. Import your audio
Use the 📂 Audio button in the top toolbar to load one or more files (.wav .mp3 .flac .ogg .aiff). Once loaded, you can optionally bring in existing annotations with 📋 Import Selections (Raven-style text/CSV) or 📊 Add Excel Selection Table (Train/Motif tables exported from Temporal Analysis). Tag the recording with a 🏷 Specimen ID (also in the toolbar) if you plan to pool several recordings later in Summarize.
2. Preprocessing
Trim, filter, resample and clean up a recording before analysis. Batch actions apply to whatever's checked in the Loaded Audio list, and edits can be saved back out.
3. Temporal Analysis
Detect peaks/pulses from the envelope and organize them into Trains and Motifs. Results tabs (Peak / Train / Motif / MotifSeq / Summary) can be exported together as one Excel workbook.
4. Spectral Analysis
Inspect the waveform and spectrogram, drag selections or send detections over from Temporal Analysis, then run 🔬 Spectral Metrics to measure and export frequency features.
5. Plotting
Render publication-ready figures, with sub-tabs for Multiplot (waveform/spectrogram combos), Osc. Stack, Osc. Zoom and Habitus plots. Presets let you save/recall your layout settings.
6. Summarize
No audio needed here — load several Temporal/Spectral Analysis .xlsx exports (one animal or several), merge them into one workbook, and get pooled/per-individual stats plus a draft text report.
